# Pedigree & Certification for Indonesian Songbirds (Burung Kicau)

Research notes (Oct 2026). Every claim has a source URL. "unverified" = no reliable source found in this pass.
Not legal advice: check with the local BKSDA before relying on any of this.

## (a) Breeding rings, permits and transport documents

### Leg rings (ring kaki / ring penangkar)
- A closed ring goes on a chick while its foot is still small and can't come off once the foot has grown. Source: https://bris.linnaeus.naturalis.nl/linnaeus_ng/app/views/glossary/term.php?id=37415 (general ornithology, not Indonesia-specific).
- Breeders often put a ring/brand code in their business name, e.g. "Ring Zahra SGR BF" (Brebes): https://idalamat.com/alamat/704047/penangkaran-muray-batu-ring-zahra-sgr-bf-brebes-jawa-tengah. What "BF" means is **unverified**.
- In 2018 KLHK said contest birds should come from licensed sources and carry a penangkaran ring mark: https://news.republika.co.id/berita/pd3mb7284/klhk-pemelihara-dan-penangkar-burung-tak-dipidana
- **Unverified:** a standard national ring format (breeder code + year + serial). We found no official spec. In practice the format appears to be set by each breeder or ring maker. Ask the BKSDA or Ditjen KSDAE.

### Protected-species list
- Permen LHK P.20/2018 (Jul 2018) added murai batu, cucak rawa, cucak ijo and jalak suren to the protected list, and kicau mania protested: https://news.detik.com/jawatengah/4166754/burung-ocehan-masuk-satwa-dilindungi-kicau-mania-protes
- In Sept 2018 the ministry removed kucica hutan (murai batu), cucak rawa and jalak suren again: https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2018/09/08/three-songbirds-removed-from-protected-list , https://mongabay.co.id/2018/09/14/tiga-jenis-burung-hendak-dikeluarkan-dari-daftar-satwa-dilindungi-berikut-penjelasan-klhk/
- Which regulation did it is not consistent across sources. Mongabay credits **P.92/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/8/2018** (https://mongabay.co.id/2021/02/28/diburu-nasib-murai-batu-makin-tak-menentu/). **P.106/MENLHK/SETJEN/KUM.1/12/2018** is the second amendment to P.20/2018 and keeps that status (https://digilib.uin-suka.ac.id/id/eprint/50181/ , https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/cara-ternak-murai-batu-untuk-pemula-izin-penangkaran-indukan-pakan-dan-hitungan-biayanya).
- **Cucak ijo stayed protected** under P.106/2018. **Murai batu and lovebird are not on that list.** Both are CITES Appendix II: https://ejurnal.undana.ac.id/index.php/warnalestari/article/download/25533/9353
- Murai batu moved to CITES Appendix II on 23 Feb 2023, so exports need a CITES permit: https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/cara-ternak-murai-batu-untuk-pemula-izin-penangkaran-indukan-pakan-dan-hitungan-biayanya
- Kenari (canary) is a domesticated non-native species and not on P.106: **unverified** (no source checked).
- Our sources do not say P.106/2018 has been replaced. Treat it as current: **unverified**.

### Captive-breeding permits (penangkaran)
- The old rule was **Permenhut P.19/Menhut-II/2005** on Penangkaran Tumbuhan dan Satwa Liar: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Search?jenis=163
- **Permen LHK No. 18 Tahun 2024** (enacted 16 Oct 2024, promulgated 18 Oct 2024) is the current rule. It covers penangkaran, keeping for hobby, trade and display: https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/309468 , https://pasal.id/peraturan/permen/permen-lhk-no-18-tahun-2024
- Under 18/2024 permits can now be processed at the BKSDA level, with no wait for central approval. Breeders must keep a logbook, a **studbook** and an annual plan (RKT). Breeding stock (indukan) needs documented legal origin: https://mongabay.co.id/2025/11/02/izin-penangkaran-satwa-endemik-dipermudah-pengawasannya/
- Commercial breeding needs a business permit through OSS (Cipta Kerja), same source. KBLI 01724 is the code for bird-breeding businesses: https://news.sah.co.id/?p=4764
- Whether 18/2024 formally revokes P.19/2005 is **unverified**. Mongabay only says the 2005 framework was "revised in 2024".

### Transport permits (SATS-DN)
- SATS-DN (Surat Angkut Tumbuhan dan Satwa Liar Dalam Negeri) is issued by the BBKSDA/BKSDA. Its legal basis is Permen LHK 18/2024 Pasal 157(1)-(2). It applies **even to non-protected species**: https://mongabay.co.id/2026/02/09/bksda-bali-gagalkan-penyelundupan-ribuan-burung-modusnya/
- Moving birds between islands also needs a quarantine health certificate: https://rri.co.id/denpasar/regional/2622027/gagal-diselundupkan-ke-pulau-jawa-bksda-bali-amankan-284-burung-di-gilimanuk
- For captive-bred birds, the SATS-DN application needs the **sertifikat penangkaran**: https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/cara-ternak-murai-batu-untuk-pemula-izin-penangkaran-indukan-pakan-dan-hitungan-biayanya
- BKSDA service standard for commercial SATS-DN: https://sippn.menpan.go.id/images/article/temp/BBKSDA_PB%20Standar%20Pelayanan%20Penerbitan%20SATSDN%20Tujuan%20Komersial-20240530155931.pdf
- In 2026 the Ministry of Forestry launched the PeSATS e-portal for SATS-DN/LN. Issuance time dropped from 1-3 months to 1-3 working days: https://rri.co.id/jakarta/info-kementerian/2615556/melalui-digitalisasi-kemenhut-tutup-celah-perdagangan-ilegal-satwa-liar

## (b) Contest certificates, piagam and trophies
- **PBI (Pelestari Burung Indonesia)** exists and runs contests through regional branches:
  - PBI Kediri, Piala Kerajaan Kediri: https://beritajatim.com/?p=636196
  - PBI Blitar, Proklamator Cup (national, 2022): https://jatimtimes.com/baca/267186/20220605/132400/hadiri-lomba-kicau-burung-nasional-proklamator-cup-bupati-mak-rini-ajak-lestarikan-keanekaragaman-hayati

## (c) How lineage is recorded
- Permen LHK 18/2024 requires breeders to keep a **studbook** and **logbook**: https://mongabay.co.id/2025/11/02/izin-penangkaran-satwa-endemik-dipermudah-pengawasannya/
- Buyers are told to buy only from breeders who can show "sertifikat dan asal indukan", and to ask for "bukti trah" when buying expensive champion lines. Breeders are told to log the dates of eggs, hatching and weaning, and the number of chicks: https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/cara-ternak-murai-batu-untuk-pemula-izin-penangkaran-indukan-pakan-dan-hitungan-biayanya
- A standard pedigree-certificate layout (sire/dam ring numbers, breeder name) used across the hobby: **unverified**. No national kicau pedigree registry was found. In practice lineage seems to be the breeder's own claim, backed by ring numbers and breeder paperwork (same caveat).

## Public verification
We searched for public pages where anyone can look up a bird document. **None were found.**
- **BKSDA permits / SATS-DN: no public check found.** PeSATS (launched Jul 2026) is an *application* portal. It checks documents internally and rejects applications automatically, but none of the coverage describes a public lookup or QR check. The coverage gives no URL either. Sources: https://rri.co.id/jakarta/info-kementerian/2615556/melalui-digitalisasi-kemenhut-tutup-celah-perdagangan-ilegal-satwa-liar , https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/KXyCWr9J-kemenhut-digitalisasi-perizinan-angkut-satwa-dan-tumbuhan-lewat-pesats . SIPPN (sippn.menpan.go.id) only hosts service-standard PDFs, not a permit lookup.
- **PBI / contest certificates: no public check found.**
- **Breeder rings: no public registry found.**

How Kicau handles this gap (it still does not verify paper):
1. **Organizer attestations.** A verified organizer can call the existing on-chain `attest()` with `CERT:<type>:<number>`, e.g. `CERT:contest_certificate:PBI-KDR-2026-0012`. That entry then shows as organizer-attested, not self-declared.
2. **Optional `docHash`.** The browser computes a SHA-256 fingerprint of a certificate photo or PDF (Web Crypto). Only the hash is stored and the file is never uploaded. The hash proves the owner's file hasn't changed since; it does not prove the document is authentic.

## How Kicau maps this

Kicau stores **owner claims only**. It does **not** verify paper documents such as rings, BKSDA permits, SATS-DN or piagam. The profile fields are:

```ts
certificates: {
  type: "breeder_ring" | "bksda_permit" | "contest_certificate" | "other";
  issuer: string;   // breeder name / BKSDA unit / PBI branch / organizer
  number: string;   // ring no., permit/SATS-DN no., certificate no.
  date: string;
  note?: string;
}[];
lineage: {
  sire?: { name: string; ring: string };   // indukan jantan
  dam?:  { name: string; ring: string };   // indukan betina
  breeder?: string;                         // penangkar
  breederRing?: string;                     // ring code/brand of the penangkar
};
wins: { event: string; organizer: string; class: string; rank: number; date: string; location: string }[];
```

Each field is one of two kinds:
- **Self-declared**: anything the owner types in, including every `certificates` and `lineage` entry and manually added `wins`. The UI shows these as claims.
- **Verified on-chain**: a `wins` entry backed by an **organizer attestation** or an **on-chain contest result** from an organizer wallet. Only these get a verified badge.

Mapping: ring kaki goes to `breeder_ring`. Izin penangkaran and SATS-DN go to `bksda_permit`; the doc type goes in `note`. PBI and other piagam go to `contest_certificate`. Studbook data goes to `lineage`.
